Semantic-Grounded VLA for Humanoid Manipulation

Real humanoid · π0 · Language-conditioned manipulation

Fine-tuned VLAs can rely on visual shortcuts rather than the language instruction. SGA-VLA strengthens instruction–target–action alignment under limited robot data.

02 / Manipulation

Occlusion-Aware Robotic Manipulation

UR10e · RGB-D · Dense, cluttered scenes

An end-to-end picking system that connects grasp-relevant instance segmentation and occlusion estimation with 3D reasoning and adaptive robot actions.

Built: perception pipeline, grasp prioritization, and direct-grasp / push-before-grasp execution on hardware.

Instance segmentationPoint cloudsGrasp planningSystem integration

03 / Dynamic manipulation

Dynamic Picking and Automated Packaging

Delta robot · Conveyor tracking · Full process

A complete automation system that detects and localizes moving objects, synchronizes robot motion, performs dynamic grasps, and coordinates downstream packaging.

Built: ROS integration, robot communication, 3D localization, motion synchronization, and packaging coordination.

ROS3D visionDynamic graspingAutomation

04 / Mobile robotics

Autonomous Navigation with Trailer-Aware Planning

Real vehicle · 3D perception · Planning and control

A navigation stack designed around the motion and footprint of the complete tractor–trailer system rather than the tractor alone.

Built: semantic 3D perception, trailer-referenced Frenet planning, obstacle avoidance, and Stanley-based tracking with real-vehicle validation.
