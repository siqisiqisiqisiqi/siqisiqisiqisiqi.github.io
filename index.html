01 / System ownership

Humanoid Upper-Limb VLA System

Built the initial upper-limb manipulation stack for a humanoid robot, spanning VR teleoperation, demonstration collection, dataset construction, policy training, and on-robot inference.

  • Developed the upper-limb inverse-kinematics solver and VR teleoperation interface.
  • Deployed and evaluated π0, ACT, and SmolVLA on bottle placement, pouring, and tissue retrieval tasks.

02 / VLA research

Semantic-Guided Action Grounding for VLA Manipulation

Proposed SGA-VLA to reduce visual shortcuts and instruction–action mismatch when fine-tuning VLA policies with limited robot data.

  • Combined target-aware visual token replacement, phase-aware attention bias, and attention supervision.
  • Ran controlled comparisons and ablations on a real humanoid robot and in RoboTwin 2.0.

03 / Perception to action

Occlusion-Aware Robotic Picking

Built a perception-guided picking system for densely occluded objects, integrating instance segmentation, 3D point clouds, grasp prioritization, and adaptive push-before-grasp behavior.
